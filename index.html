Materials, Fluting & Embellishments

Tailored industrial substrates designed for structural integrity and tactile brand differentiation.

▦

Substrates & Board Stock

Sourced exclusively from certified mills with traceable chain-of-custody.

  • Virgin Kraft: Maximum tear resistance & rich earthy aesthetic
  • Bleached White SBS: 94+ brightness for ultra-vivid CMYK printing
  • Recycled Testliner: 100% post-consumer sustainable corrugated
  • Rigid Greyboard: High-density 1200–1800 GSM structural core
≈

Fluting Architecture

Calibrated fluting thickness matching your product weight and transit profile.

  • E-Flute (1.5mm): Tight wave density; ideal for pristine litho printing
  • B-Flute (3.0mm): High puncture resistance; standard for D2C parcel shipping
  • C-Flute (4.0mm): Superior stacking compression for warehouse logistics
  • BC-Flute (Double Wall 6.5mm): Heavy freight, electronics & machinery
✦

Finishes & Embellishments

Specialty artisanal treatments applied directly on high-speed press runs.

  • Soft-Touch Velvet: Luxurious silky tactile coating with zero glare
  • High-Gloss Spot UV: High-contrast mirror finish on key logos and motifs
  • Hot Foil Stamping: Metallic gold, silver, rose gold, and holographic foils
  • Blind Deboss / Emboss: Dramatic tactile depth without ink bleed
